Digital Identity Mechanics

Discover how your recorded actions are reviewed by the community and how they translate into a unique, visual representation of your journey. This section breaks down the STK evaluation and profile system.

1

The Rule of 7 Reviewers

Every personal action video you upload is reviewed by 7 randomly selected users. Approvals require no comment, but Denials require a constructive explanation. Once voting ends, a majority approval earns you 1 point in the corresponding Path.

Result: Pending Review
2

Your RGB Identity

There's no limit to growth. As you earn points across the three Paths, they blend into Red, Green, and Blue color channels. This creates a unique color identity that visually reflects your specific journey and focus areas.

Real-World Connections

STK extends beyond the digital screen. It recognizes the power of teamwork for larger challenges and facilitates honest feedback during physical interactions. Here is how groups and real-life encounters work.

👥 Group Challenges

Some actions need a team of 10, 20, or more. STK allows Group Registration (requires a domain email). Groups solve challenges and earn points based on how many people view the recorded challenge.

It’s a healthy competition where the goal is to care for all living beings.
