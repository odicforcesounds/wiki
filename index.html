Social Tool Kit (STK)

Comprehensive Platform Documentation & Rulebook

1. Introduction

The **Social Tool Kit (STK)** is a platform designed to validate actions, promote positive behavior, and create meaningful impact across communities worldwide. It is more than a social network; it is a global decentralized ledger of social proof, built for individuals aged 13 and above who are dedicated to improving themselves and their societies.

STK empowers individuals to validate each other's actions in different contexts, known as **(Paths)**:

  • Human Care: Actions focusing on social support, education, empathy, mentorship, and collective well-being.
  • Nature Care: Actions focusing on environmental care, conservation, cleanup, and awareness.
  • Animal Care: Actions concerning pet welfare, stray animal rescue, wildlife protection, and habitat preservation.

These recorded interactions do not merely sit in a profile; they actively shape how users are viewed during different moments of their real-time performance. In STK, your profile is a living, breathing metric of your integrity as seen through the eyes of others. Users develop deep, multi-faceted personal qualities within various environmental contexts—such as Family, School, Work, Business, and Civism, providing a complete picture of their contribution and performance in community-driven activities.

2. Your Ethical Signature

In STK, your profile is not a collection of static information. It is your **Ethical Signature**. This signature is a dynamic, verifiable snapshot of your real-world integrity, performance, and contribution history. It visualizes your standing as a responsible community member.

Your signature displays your unique Ethical Title (e.g., Architect, Weaver, Catalyst), your Architect Level, and your current point standing within the three central Paths.

To provide video authenticity, the system uses your YouTube account to validate that shared action videos are actively manage by you, linking your digital footprint to your human actions.

3. Personal Characteristics System

The Personal Characteristics System (Verify Feedback) is prioritized as the primary method of gathering real-time data on your social activity and community contribution, as seen through the eyes of traditional systems completely overlook.

This system empowers individuals to validate each other's real-time performances during personal, real-world interactions. By providing feedback, you help the entire community build a trustworthy network. These values represent who you are in others' eyes and can provide invaluable self-reflection, helping you understand area where you wish to refine your social activity and become a better version of yourself.

3.1. Fundamental Rules of Consent

Strict Anti-Harassment & Integrity Protocol:

To ensure total respect and the integrity of the data, the Personal Characteristics System is bound by strict protocols:

  • You cannot search for random users to rate them. All ratings require physical proximity.
  • You must meet the user personally in the physical world. Consent is verified by asking them to share their unique **QR code**.
  • You must perform the rating on your device using your account after scanning their code.
  • STK has no chat or messaging system. You cannot contact other users directly through the platform.
  • You can only add one comment per video, providing context rather than initiating private conversation.
  • Rating limits are strictly enforced: you can only rate a maximum of 3 personal characteristics per day, per user. This prevents spamming and manipulative feedback.

3.2. Rating Activity: Performance by Context

You can rate performances by context, choosing from multiple distinct environments, including Family, School, Work, Business, and Civism. A different set of at least 10 characteristic qualifiers is presented for each context (e.g., different qualities are rated for a Family member vs. a Business partner). 4. Action Validation (Videos)

STK moves beyond passive validation by requiring **Direct Proof of Action**. This is achieved by documenting your contributions using Video Validation.

We leverage your existing YouTube Account to ensure total video authenticity—this validates that the videos you share are actively managed by you, eliminating fakes and maintaining data integrity.

You decide whether your videos are Public (visible to everyone) or Unlisted (visible only to people with the link, the default for validation).

4.1. Process & Points

After recording an action, you upload it to your YouTube account. Your documentation now enters the Public Action Registry for verification.

Crucially, to maintain total decentralized objectivity, 7 random approvals are needed from anonymous STK users to validate your action. approvals are instant. upon achieving 7 approvals, the user is granted 1 Path Point (granting 1 unit of Path progression in Nature Care, Animal Care, or Human Care).

4.2. Technical Video Requirements

To ensure high standards of verification, all action documentation videos must adhere to specific Technical Requirements:

  • Action-Focused: You do not need to show your face. The video must focus purely on your actions only. The community is rating the contribution, not the person.
  • Length Constraint: Your standard action validation videos cannot be more than 5 minutes long. Keep documentation concise and focused on the complete action. (This length does not apply to Solved-Challenge videos).

5. Syndicate Challenges

Syndicate Challenges represent large-scale, cooperative collective actions where Groups play a major role. While individual actions are impactful, the collective might of groups tackling significant public issues is amplified through the Challenge mechanism. These cooperative effort are ranked with the global STK community based on community views, showcasing community strength and global impact.

Registered groups are showcased globally, highlighting meaningful contributions that positively impact communities.

5.1. Creating a Syndicate Challenge

Any individual user can create a Syndicate Challenge to identify a localized need that requires collective action.

  1. Identify the Problem: Find a critical public issue (e.g., "[Huge plastic recycling problem] in specific Park").
  2. Record Verification Video: Create a video (max 5 minutes) that documents the problem and its location. You must include complete location context (GPS coordinates, Zone, City, Country) and a title in the description (e.g., "Garbage in the Forest [Forest Name]").
  3. Public Approval: Your creation video is reviewed by 7 random users. They must approve the problem documentation to validate the Challenge.
  4. Point Incubation: Upon successful approval, the Challenge begins accumulating Challenge Points (distinct from Path points) for every user who views it. If a group solves this challenge, the original creator is recognized with 10 Path Points.

5.2. Groups Solving Challenges

Groups participate in a decentralized "impact visibility contest." Their completed efforts are ranked by the global STK community, creating a new metric of cooperative or social responsibility. Rankings of Completed challenges are structured based on community views.

6. Groups (Guilds / Community Cooperatives)

To operate at a higher level and tackle massive Syndicate Challenges, users must form or join a registered **Group**. These entities are the cooperative engines of STK. Only leaders can register their group.

6.1. Group Registration Protocol

Pre-requisites for Legitimate Groups:

  • You cannot register your group until the STK application is available on the Google Play Store and is being actively used by the registering leader.
  • Domain Authentication is mandatory: Your group must own and verify a dedicated domain with e-mail service to register. This ensures total commitment and legitimacy of the group's intent.
  • Only formal **Group Leaders** have the authority to register the group on the platform. Please be sure you have the pre-requisites before registering.

6.2. Syndicate Challenge Protocol (Cooperative Power)

Group leaders are responsible for managing challenges, validating participation, and managing the validated participation of members and uploading videos of **Solved Challenges**.

  • Locking Challenges (Consensus Drive): To prevent points being wasted, a leader can only **Lock a specific challenge** from the app (stopping point growth) if more than 50% of the active group members select it within the app. Locking indicates the group is initiating the solution.
  • Resolution documentation: After locking, group members must solve the localized problem (ex: cleanup Park) and record proof. The Solved-Challenge video can be up to 60 minutes long, providing comprehensive proof of the complete solution.

    • Rewards of Cooperation

    Upon successful resolution and Leader upload of the Solved-Challenge video:

    • Each participating **group member gains 3 Path points**, recognizing their cooperative impact.
    • The Group gains the total point value accrued by that challenge. This score promotes the group’s standing, placing them above or below others in the public global rankings, demonstrating which cooperative entities are achieving the highest public social proof.
