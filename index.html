Video Rate System ->

Your videos will be rated by 7 random users. After 7 positive feedbacks, user gain 1 point to the video-Path.
Rating Challenges follow the same logic

Personal Rate System ->

Consent: You cannot search for random users. You need to meet them personally and ask them to share their QR code to be rated by you, using your device with your account. You cannot chat or send messages to other users. You can only add one comment per video.
You can only rate 3 personal characteristics per day and per user.

Groups & Challenges ->

Only leaders can register their group. Please be sure you have the requirements. If more than 50% of members select a challenge, Leader can Lock it. No more growing points to this challenge. Solve it and record a video. Video can be 60 minutes long.
After approval, your Group name will be exposed in the list of this competition, above or below others.
