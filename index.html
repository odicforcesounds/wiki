Project Update: A New Direction is Coming

A Sincere Apology & Our Next Chapter

To our community, early adopters, and supporters of the Social Tool Kit (STK) vision: We owe you an apology.

We recently announced the cancellation of the original STK platform. We set out to build an ethical, verifiable social network, but in our pursuit of absolute security and authenticity, we built a system that was simply too hard to use.

We demanded too much friction (forcing YouTube uploads and 7-person reviews), stripped away vital social features like chat, and placed unrealistic, heavy restrictions on grassroots groups by forcing domain registrations. We forgot that tools for social good must, above all else, be accessible to everyone.

We are sorry for missing the mark. But we are not giving up on the mission.

Announcing a Brand New Application

We have gone back to the drawing board. We are currently developing a completely new application built from the ground up to fix the mistakes of the past. Here is what you can expect:

  • Zero Friction Onboarding: A radically simplified experience that respects your time. No forced external uploads or overly complex verification processes.
  • True Social Connection: Restoring normal communication features so you can actually connect, collaborate, and chat with peers who share your values.
  • Empowering Grassroots: We are removing the corporate barriers. Any group of friends, students, or neighbors will be able to easily form a group and participate in community challenges—no domain names or IT skills required.

Our core goal remains unchanged: to promote positive behavior and reward meaningful impact in Nature Care, Animal Care, and Human Care. But this time, we are making it easy, fun, and truly social.

Thank you for your patience and for holding us to a higher standard. We cannot wait to show you what we are building next. Stay tuned.
