What Civic Perks is

A game you play by walking.

Most city apps ask you to fill in a form. Civic Perks asks you to go outside. It is a community map, a scavenger hunt and a neighbourhood report card in one — with two currencies, a trust score and a jury of neighbours. Everything you do happens where you physically stand.

📍

Report the real world

Drop a pin on what needs attention and on what is still useful: hazards, free water, benches, curb furniture, books, safe bike racks, stray-cat corners and more. Each pin carries a short note, a category, a scale and the exact spot where you made it.

🤝

Confirm what neighbours find

A new pin is fragile until other scouts walk to it. Validating a pin means you were there and the thing is real — that is what makes the map trustworthy instead of another pile of anonymous complaints.

⚡

Earn XP and Impact Credits

XP tracks your reputation and level; it never decreases and never rolls dice. Impact Credits are spendable: earn them by walking, solving photo bounties and claiming quests, then trade them for boosts in the shop.

🧭

The core rule

You have to be there. Reporting, validating, solving and flagging all require live GPS, and validate / solve / flag only work within 60 metres of the pin. There is no couch-mapping, no teleport and no simulated movement. Your live position is never shown to other scouts.

How it works

From first login to first fix.

No setup, no download: open the site on your phone and the app runs full-screen like a native Android app, with a bottom tab bar, a raised + button and map-first navigation.

  1. 01

    Sign in with Google

    Google is the only sign-in method. No email and password, no anonymous accounts — your progress follows you, and fake identities stay out of the map.

  2. 02

    Turn on live location

    GPS is required, not optional. The app asks for browser permission before you play and keeps a live fix while it is open, with an accuracy chip so you know how precise you are.

  3. 03

    Finish the field tutorial

    Two real actions: drop the practice pin where you stand (+10 XP), then classify the hexagon you are in (+25 XP). That makes you Level 1 and unlocks the full pin menu.

  4. 04

    Drop pins as you move

    Tap +, pick a category and a type, choose small / medium / large, add a note. Pins are always placed at your current position, and categories unlock as you level up.

  5. 05

    Confirm your neighbours' pins

    When you are in range, open a pin and validate it, solve it when it is a rescuable item, or flag it if it is fake. Three correct flags remove a pin and cost its author trust.

  6. 06

    Walk, earn, climb

    Distance pays credits, actions pay XP, daily quests pay both. Level up, widen your map, buy boosts, and compare your week with scouts from all over the city.

The map

A neighbourhood drawn in hexagons.

The map runs on free OpenStreetMap tiles rendered with a locally bundled Leaflet — no paid map keys, and the “© OpenStreetMap contributors” attribution is always visible. In the dark theme the tiles are toned down so pins and zones stay readable; the light theme keeps them natural for daylight and astigmatism.

Pins appear as you zoom

Pins load at street level, so the map is not a wall of dots from far away. New and unconfirmed pins show a dashed, slightly faded ring; verified pins glow in their category colour. Photo resources carry a credit bounty tag. Your own position is a pulsing cyan dot with a recenter button.

Zones have a personality

The area is split into deterministic hexagons. Standing in one, you classify it and then rate it Safe or Dangerous. Your first vote pays +25 XP; later votes update the score. The outline turns green for safe and red for dangerous on everyone's map.

Two modes

Normal when the city is awake. Vanguard when it is not.

The app counts how many other scouts are live within five miles and picks the mode for you. There is no manual switch: a jury needs company.

Normal · 3+ scouts nearby

The community jury is active

Pins need independent confirmation before they count as verified.

  • New pins start at 10 confidence; each validation adds 35
  • A pin is verified at 100 — three nearby confirmations
  • Pin cap: 10 per day
  • Full validation, solving and audit flow
  • Some days include a private honesty audit (see below)
Vanguard · fewer than 3 scouts

You are the first one here

Cold-start rules keep a solo scout useful until the map has a crowd.

  • Your new pins are auto-verified but flagged for back-check
  • A different scout's confirmation clears the flag and pays normal XP
  • Pin cap: 20 per day
  • Validate / solve wait until a third scout exists
  • Walking and reporting quests still run; credits stay flat and predictable

Progression

Twenty levels, earned in the open.

XP is fixed and cumulative: the same action always pays the same XP, so a lucky new account can never out-rank a veteran. Levels unlock pin categories, wider map zoom and, at Level 5, the Weekly Civic Report. A Double XP boost doubles gains for two hours but never changes the base values.

Rewards

XP is a score. Credits are a wallet.

Two currencies with two jobs. XP measures reputation and never gets spent. Impact Credits are earned by moving and by resource bounties, then spent in the boost shop. Credits are a closed loop: they cannot be cashed out, and reporting is never locked behind a payment or a stake.

📺

Free boost, optional ad

The shop offers one sponsored video per free boost — up to 10 per day. Finishing the video grants a random 30-minute single-boost effect; skipping, closing early or declining ad consent grants nothing. Ads are never required to play and never block the map. The same boost can always be bought with credits instead. Details are in the privacy & ads policy.

Fair play

Trust is the real high score.

Anyone can drop a pin. What makes the map worth using is that the next scout had to stand in the same place and agree. The rules below are the ones you will feel most often.

⚖️

Trust score

You start at 75%, capped at 99.9%. It rises +0.2 each time another scout validates one of your pins. Reporting and validating never move your own trust. The number turns red below 80%.

🛡️

Confidence, not votes

A fresh pin starts at 10 confidence and gains 35 per independent validator. It becomes verified at 100. Three flags remove it and cost the author XP and trust. A scout can flag someone else's pin once.

⏱️

Claims and cleanups

“On my way” locks a photo pin for 10 minutes. Collect it fast for +15 XP within five minutes, or +8 XP within ten. If it is gone, say so: the stale pin is removed for +50 XP.

📌

Daily caps

You can drop 10 pins a day in Normal mode and 20 in Vanguard. Validations are unlimited but count once per pin. Photo-bounty credit earnings are capped at low levels; XP keeps flowing.

🔍

Honesty audits

Some Normal-mode days hide a private decoy pin near you. Confirming it costs 5 trust and removes it; flagging it as fake pays +5 XP and completes your validation objective.

📶

Nearby scouts

The app sends a short-lived presence heartbeat and receives only a count of other live scouts within five miles — never their coordinates. Presence expires in minutes when you close the app.

Beyond the pin

Quests, routes, ranks and reports.

Walking is the core loop, but the app also gives you a reason to pick a direction, a way to compare your week with the city, and a document you can actually show someone.

🎯

Daily quests

Walk 1 km (+30 credits), report 3 pins (+30 credits), solve 1 confirmed issue (+50 credits), and — in Normal mode — validate the one nearby pin the app picks for you (+25 credits). Rewards are credits, claimed manually, and reset at midnight.

🧭

Create a route

Name a walk and choose one of five themes — Lifestyle & Social, History & Lore, Culinary & Nightlife, Art & Architecture, Scenic & Nature. Publish with 3 to 15 stops within six hours; every pin you drop while the route is open becomes a stop.

🏆

Leaderboards

All-time XP, weekly XP (resets Monday), monthly XP and friends. Your own row is always shown even when you are far from the top, and ties break on trust score — not on who joined first.

📄

Weekly Civic Report

From Level 5, once every seven days, export a PDF of your last week: pins, validations, zones, distance and impact. It carries a QR code that opens a public /verified record. The online record is canonical and confirms Civic Perks issued it; it does not independently audit self-reported actions.

Built on open foundations

No download. No lock-in. No paid map.

Civic Perks is a single-page mobile web app, built to feel like a native Android app and hosted on the open web at civic-perks.org.

OpenStreetMap + Leaflet

Free community map data, with the library bundled locally rather than loaded from a CDN. Attribution stays on screen at all times.

Google sign-in, Firebase auth

One identity, no password database. The app is gated on a real Google session, and only Firebase can authenticate production writes.

Your progress, your device

Game state is stored in your browser's local storage and can be reset in Profile → Developer. Shared pins and zone votes are kept on the community map. Read the privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions before you walk out the door.

Is Civic Perks free?

Yes. The app is free to use, reporting is never paywalled, and Impact Credits are a closed in-game currency with no cash value and no cash-out. The only monetisation is the optional rewarded video that can unlock a free boost.

Do I have to live in Porto?

The starter map is seeded around Bonfim, Porto, because that is where the app was built and tested. Pins are always placed at your live position, so you can use the app wherever you are — the community map simply starts empty in a new area.

Why does the app need my exact location?

Because physical presence is the whole point. Reporting, validating, solving and flagging depend on where you actually stand, within 60 metres. Without a live GPS fix the app deliberately does not let you play; there is no approximate-area fallback. Only pin coordinates and a short-lived presence count are shared — other scouts never see your live position.

Why can't I validate a pin I can see?

Two possible reasons. You are probably not within 60 metres of it yet — walk closer and the action enables. Or you are in Vanguard mode, where validation stays inactive until at least three scouts are live nearby and a jury is possible.

Why did my new pin not count as verified straight away?

In Normal mode a new pin is provisional: it starts at 10 confidence and needs independent confirmations to reach 100. In Vanguard mode your pins are auto-verified but marked for back-check until another scout confirms them.

Can I sign in with email or play as a guest?

No. Google is the only production sign-in method, and there is no anonymous mode. A test account exists for local development only and is clearly marked TEST in the profile.

What happens if I report something fake?

Three flags from other scouts remove the pin, and the author loses XP and trust. Deliberately confirming an honesty-audit decoy costs 5 trust. The map is only useful if the next person can trust it.

Does the app track me in the background?

No. Location is requested while the app is open and used for pinning, proximity and distance credits. Presence heartbeats expire after about 15 minutes. Closing the tab ends the session.

How do I start over?

Profile → Developer → Reset progress clears the local game state on that device. If you clear your browser's site data instead, the app treats you as a new scout.

Is there a native Android app?

Not yet. The project deliberately ships as a mobile web app that installs from the browser and behaves like the Android app it is modelled on — same shell, same map, same rules.
